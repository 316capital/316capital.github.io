Rental and DSCR loans

Long term financing for income producing rental property.

DSCR loans qualify on the property debt service coverage ratio, meaning the rent the property produces against the payment, rather than on the borrower personal income or tax returns. That makes them workable for investors whose returns show heavy depreciation.

Available for purchase, rate and term refinance, and cash out refinance.
