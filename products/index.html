Loan products for real estate investors

316 Capital arranges and places four core loan products for real estate investors.

Fix and flip: purchase plus rehab financing with draw based rehab funding, for investors renovating and reselling residential property.

BRRRR: financing structured for buy, rehab, rent, refinance, repeat strategies.

New construction: ground up financing for new residential construction, from land through vertical build.

Rental and DSCR: long term financing for income producing rental property, qualified on the property cash flow rather than the borrower tax returns.

All loans are business purpose only. We do not finance owner occupied primary residences.
