BRRRR financing

Financing structured for the buy, rehab, rent, refinance, repeat strategy.

The strategy needs two loans that fit together: short term acquisition and rehab capital on the front end, then a long term DSCR refinance once the property is stabilised and seasoned.

Handling both sides through one point of contact means the exit is planned before the first loan closes, rather than after.
